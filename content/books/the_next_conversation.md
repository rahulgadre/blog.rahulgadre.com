There are several books about communication which show us how to communicate. Seldom do we find books that teach us what to say. The Next Conversation is one of those rare books that teaches us both what to say and how to communicate with people. I came across this book while searching online for books on communication. The core idea of the book is to help you get better with your next conversation.

The book is filled with ideas, pointers, and tidbits which are practical. As the author says, it may not be feasible to implement all techniques at once; he encourages readers to start with one. Once that one technique becomes a part of your communication, move on to the next one.

Throughout the book, we learn several practical lessons including pausing, understanding the core issue rather than winning an argument, and conflict management. The book also teaches how to show assertiveness, speak confidently, set boundaries, and respond to insults. The best part is there are ready-to-use lines from different sections which you can immediately start using in your next conversation.

There is a lot to learn from the book and I am glad I came across this one. Considering all the self-improvement books I read, this one, in particular, holds a place near the top.

**My rating: 9.5 / 10**
